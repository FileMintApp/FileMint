# FileMint visual brief

Tool: built-in ImageGen, used for logo-brand concept generation.
Reference: FileMint-Concept.png. Production assets: native AppKit vector drawing.

## Concept prompt

Create one completely new, memorable, polished macOS app icon for FileMint, a
small native tool that instantly creates files. The central symbol is a custom
uppercase F made from one folded sheet of mint-colored paper: a strong vertical
stem, a generous top arm with one folded upper-right corner, and a shorter
middle arm. It should read as an F and a fresh file, with a clear silhouette.
Use subtly rounded edges, a mint-to-jade material, restrained dimensional light,
and a delicate cast shadow on a warm porcelain rounded-square tile. Straight-on
view, simple enough for 32 pixels. No plus sign, medical cross, leaf, folder,
sparkles, lettering, glass bubbles, icon grid or surrounding objects.

## Production adaptation

The ImageGen concept establishes the composition and folded F direction. The
shipping icon is drawn independently in AppKit to guarantee smooth boundaries
and reproducible pixel sizes. It is not a bitmap cleanup or edit of the concept.
Small toolbar assets use a larger, flattened F with a negative-space folded
corner; they inherit system tint and contain no baked-in background.
